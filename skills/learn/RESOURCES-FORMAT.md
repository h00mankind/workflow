# RESOURCES.md format

The curated, trusted sources for this topic. Lesson knowledge comes from here, not from parametric guesses; wisdom comes from the communities listed here.

```md
# {Topic} resources

## Knowledge
- [Book/Article/Doc: title — author](url)
  One line: what it covers, and when to reach for it.

## Communities
- [name](url)
  One line: what it's good for (critique, troubleshooting, feedback).

## Gaps
- {Areas the mission needs that no good source covers yet — drives future search}
```

Rules:

- **High-trust only.** Primary sources, recognized experts, well-moderated communities. Marketing dressed as education stays out.
- **Annotate every entry** — a bare link is useless in three months.
- **Prune ruthlessly.** A source that proved wrong or shallow gets removed, not buried. Five sharp sources beat thirty mediocre ones.
- **Respect community preferences.** If the user doesn't want to join communities, note it here and stop proposing them.
