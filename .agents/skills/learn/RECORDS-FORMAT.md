# RECORDS.md format

The teaching equivalent of decision records: dated entries, newest last, capturing decision-grade insights that steer future sessions. This is what the zone of proximal development is calculated from.

```md
## YYYY-MM-DD — {Short title of what was learned or established}
{1–3 sentences: what was learned, and why it changes what to teach next.}
```

Write an entry when:

1. **The user demonstrated genuine understanding** of something non-trivial — evidence they can use it, not just exposure to it. Sets a new floor for what to teach next.
2. **The user disclosed prior knowledge** — "I already know X." Record it (and the claimed depth) so it isn't re-taught.
3. **A misconception was corrected** — high value; these predict stumbling blocks in related topics.
4. **The mission shifted** — record the shift and update `MISSION.md`.

What does *not* qualify:

- Material merely covered. Coverage isn't learning — wait for evidence.
- Term definitions — those go in `GLOSSARY.md`.
- Session journals. Records are insights, not activity logs.

When a later entry contradicts an earlier one, append the new entry and mark the old one `(superseded YYYY-MM-DD)` rather than deleting it — how understanding evolved is itself signal.
